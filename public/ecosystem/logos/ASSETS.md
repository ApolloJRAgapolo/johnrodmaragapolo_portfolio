# Professional Ecosystem logos

These assets are used only by the Professional Ecosystem section. Original files in Downloads are unchanged.

- `isatu.webp`, `san-miguel.webp`, `iloilo-province.webp`, and `wadhwani.webp`: lossless, resized copies of the supplied transparent PNGs. Transparent outer padding is trimmed; white artwork inside official seals is preserved.
- `analytica.webp`: lossless rendering of the supplied ANALYTICA SVG, with transparent outer padding trimmed.
- `tumanow.svg`: supplied vector artwork with its viewport cropped to the artwork bounds.
- `tumanow-dark.svg`: the same geometry and gold artwork; navy fills use off-white for contrast on the dark theme.
- `kwadra.svg` / `kwadra.webp`: a flat vector trace of the supplied geometric KWADRA mark, with transparent gaps and background. Blue and gold are preserved; automatic raster cutouts were rejected for introducing gradients.
- `careertrack.webp`: lossless, resized rendering of the supplied `careertrack logo.svg`, with its transparent viewport padding trimmed. The original navy, slate, and gold artwork is preserved.
- `pricepulse.svg`: supplied `PricePulse_PH_Logo.svg` vector artwork with its viewport cropped to the artwork bounds and a transparent edge gutter.
- `pricepulse-dark.svg`: the same vector geometry and blue artwork; the navy wordmark uses off-white for contrast on the dark theme.

The personal experience card reuses the existing portfolio portrait at `/badges/my portfolio profile pic.png`, displayed as a circular crop without changing the source file.

The logo files identify the institutions and teams in documented education, project, internship, or participation contexts. They do not indicate endorsement or employment.
